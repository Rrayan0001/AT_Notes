# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Engineering students ( chiefly the note author's study group ) who read scanned
handwritten IT notes — networking, cloud, DevOps, AWS — and someone in "study
mode": a browser, the notebook scan beside the answer, one question at a time.

## Product Purpose

An authenticated, domain-scoped Q&A "study desk" over transcribed handwritten
notebooks. The user asks a question; the answer arrives cited to the exact
scanned page it came from. Success = the user trusts the answer because the
scan sits beside it and the transcription's own words are preserved verbatim.

## Brand Personality

Composed. Exacting. Quiet. The confidence of a lab report. Short declarative
sentences, numbers over adjectives, limits stated before benefits.

## Anti-references

- Neo-brutalism: thick borders, offset shadows, sticker/badge grids.
- Generic AI product: neon, gradients, glassmorphism, chat-bubble UI, typing dots, glowing edges, robot/brain iconography.
- A page that could be mistaken for any SaaS dashboard: uniform card grids, centered everything, icon-plus-paragraph triplets.
- Emoji, mascots, "AI-powered" labelling, exclamation marks.

## Design Principles

1. **Provenance is visible.** Serif = the notebook (verbatim), sans = the assistant, mono = the machine. Never merged.
2. **Allowed to say no.** An answer that isn't in the notes says so, in its own labelled block.
3. **Numbers, not adjectives.** Corpus facts, retrieval scores, and limits are stated as data in mono.
4. **Paper, not chrome.** Warm paper ground, hairline rules, one accent (Rubric) per screen. Nothing lifts; nothing glows.
5. **One email = one domain.** The signup choice is permanent and everything after it is scoped to it — the interface states limits plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, visible 1px Rubric focus outline with 2px
offset on every interactive element (instant, never fading), keyboard-operable
citations and forms, prefers-reduced-motion honored (everything instant, still
present).
