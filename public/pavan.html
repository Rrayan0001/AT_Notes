    About this notebook

    This is a spiral-bound notebook of handwritten IT study notes, eighty-two pages, scanned page by page. Every word you can read here was transcribed by hand from that scan, and the transcription is deliberately unfaithful in exactly one way: it keeps the author's mistakes.

    What is preserved

    • Original spelling and capitalisation, including chearty, MACI address and Parcmiko.
    • Grammatical errors, incomplete sentences, and fragments the author abandoned mid-thought.
    • Wrong facts, wrong formulas and broken code, reproduced rather than repaired.
    • Struck-through text, marked with strikethrough.
    • Non-English content, including the Devanagari margin note on page 2.

    Known physical defects in the source

    Page 31 is blank in the scan. Page 81 has its right edge physically clipped, and two lines are cut off mid-word. Page 74 abandons the multi-head-attention sentence at the page bottom. Page 82 ends on the single word With, roughly sixty percent of the page empty. Page 6 carries an ambiguous numeral before VMs, readable as either 2 or x.

    How it is searched

    Passages are split by heading rather than by a fixed window, so a subsection stays with its parent. Each passage is embedded and kept beside its page, its heading and its path in the notebook. A question is run against a keyword index and a vector index at once, the two rankings are fused, and the survivors are re-ordered by a second model that is allowed to give up — if it runs long, the original order is used instead. The answer is then written from the retrieved passages only, and every claim carries the page it came from.

    When the notebook cannot answer, the system says so. That refusal is not a failure; it is the part of the design that makes the rest of it trustworthy.
